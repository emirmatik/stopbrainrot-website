Privacy Policy for Stop Brain Rot

Last updated: February 2026

Introduction

Stop Brain Rot (“the app”) helps you block selected apps during focus periods using Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) APIs. This policy describes what data the app uses and where it is stored.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal data on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or third‑party SDKs that collect data.
  • No account or login is required to use the app.

Data stored on your device

All data stays on your device (and in Apple’s systems where stated below):

  • Blocks and app groups: Block names, times, days, and which apps are in each group are stored only on your device (in the app’s container and shared with the app’s extensions via an App Group).
  • App selection (Screen Time): When you choose which apps to block, that selection is managed by Apple’s Family Controls framework and stored on your device. We do not receive or store this list on any server.
  • Subscription status: If you subscribe, Apple processes the purchase. The app stores only whether you have an active subscription and a transaction identifier locally so it can unlock features. We do not receive your payment details or identity.

Apple

  • Screen Time / Family Controls are provided by Apple. Use of those features is subject to Apple’s privacy policy and terms.
  • In‑app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchase and subscription data: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
